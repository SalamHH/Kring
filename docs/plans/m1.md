# M1 — API, matching, push, Expo screens

Spec: [`docs/kring-spec.md`](../kring-spec.md) §6, §7, §8.5–8.8, §14. M0 report: [`docs/m0-report.md`](../m0-report.md).
Definition of done (§14): internal build (TestFlight + Play internal); push ≤ 2 min end-to-end.

**Settings for this milestone:**
- **Scope:** ingestion stays Oslo (0301) and Akershus (county 32).
- **Where it runs:** Render (web service, background worker, managed Postgres + PostGIS), moved here from M0. Local setup is unchanged.
- **Mobile:** Expo (TypeScript, expo-router) in `mobile/`, as in §8.9, with MapLibre and Kartverket map tiles (D11). Push and MapLibre need a development build, not Expo Go.
- **Out of scope (M2):** the weekly digest job and the digest card on Hjem, the settings screen, the «delete all data» screen, the privacy policy and store listings. `DELETE /v1/devices/me` is still built in M1 (step 3), because it is part of the API.
- **Out of scope (M3):** more Akershus precision (M0 open issue 2: per-municipality aliases, more place names from text, DATEX II road data) and road-only messages (M0 open issue 3, D4). The Akershus regression floor in the quality gate stays.
- **Your track:** Apple Developer and Google Play Console accounts, an Expo account, and a Render account with a payment method. Step 4 needs Render; step 8 needs the Expo account, a phone build on iPhone needs the Apple Developer account (a paid membership, so sign up early), and step 12 needs the rest.

Each backend step is one commit and ends with `uv run pytest` and `uv run ruff check .` passing. Each mobile step ends with `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx expo lint` and `npx jest` passing.

## Design choices

Decided 2026-11-13 (written into the spec in step 0):

1. **D1 — Town-level and municipality-only events:** the spec as written.
   - Town-level events with a point are «imprecise nearby» only, and are never pushed.
   - Municipality-only events are not listed for places. They only count towards the bydel note on the place card.
2. **D2 — District-wide notices** (§15 question 8): stored and shown in-app on Hjem for 24 h to devices with a place in the district.
   - Savnet notices are also pushed, under all of the place's rules (follows Savnet, push on, ≤ 30 min old, inside the time window, < 3 pushes today), at most once per device.
   - Other notices are never pushed.
3. **D3 — Re-geocode:** a manual command, dry run by default, `--apply` to write. It never pushes, and it is run by hand after geocoder changes.
4. **D4 — Road-only messages:** deferred to M3.
5. **D5 — Incident title:** category + place, from our own fields («Brann · Hølandsgata»). Never police text.
6. **D6 — Push body:** no police text. Only place, category, status and precision.
7. **D11 — Maps:** MapLibre with Kartverket tiles instead of react-native-maps (a change to §8.1).

Also decided 2026-11-13 (proposed defaults, approved as is):

8. **D7 — Detection time and where matching runs:** «detected» = when the worker first stored the event (new `events.ingested_at`). Matching and push run in the worker, after the ingest transaction commits.
9. **D8 — Render:** starter paid tiers for web, worker and Postgres, in Frankfurt (EU data). Render has no free background worker. Its free Postgres expires, and its free web service sleeps.
10. **D9 — Places outside Oslo and Akershus:** allowed, with a note in Nytt sted: «Vi dekker foreløpig Oslo og Akershus».
11. **D10 — Places per device:** at most 10, to stop abuse. §11 keeps «more than 3» as a later premium idea, so no 3-place limit in the beta.
12. **D12 — Hendelse actions:** «Del med familien» opens the OS share sheet with the text and source, and no link. «Færre slike varsler» opens the place's edit screen at categories.
